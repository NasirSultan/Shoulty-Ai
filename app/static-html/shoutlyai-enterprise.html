Enterprise features

Everything in the standard plan — plus everything large teams need.

Enterprise includes every feature from the standard plan (all AI tools, all 10 platforms, full analytics, brand kit, brand assets, bulk scheduling) plus these additional capabilities built specifically for large teams and agencies.

👥
Custom team seats
Add exactly the number of seats your team needs — no forced bundles. Pay for 7 seats, 14 seats, or 200 seats. Assign roles: Admin, Manager, Creator, Viewer, or Client. Role-based permissions control what each team member can see, edit, approve, and publish. Junior staff get Creator access; clients get read-only Viewer access; senior staff get Approval authority.
Role-based access control
📚
Unlimited workspaces — full client isolation
Create a completely isolated workspace for every client or brand. Each workspace has its own Brand Kit, content calendar, analytics, brand assets, and team access. There is zero cross-contamination between workspaces — a team member on Client A’s workspace cannot see or access Client B. Clients can be granted view-only access to their own workspace for direct approval without needing a Shoutly AI seat.
Zero brand bleed between clients
🌟
White-label platform
Run Shoutly AI under your own brand. Your agency logo replaces Shoutly AI in the interface. Your domain (e.g. app.youragency.com) serves the platform. Custom colour scheme matches your brand. Email notifications come from your domain. Client approval links show your branding, not Shoutly AI’s. Your clients think they’re using a proprietary tool you built — because to them, they are.
Custom domain + logo + colours
🔒
Single Sign-On (SSO)
Authenticate your entire team through your organisation’s existing identity provider. Supports SAML 2.0 (Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin, Ping Identity) and Google Workspace SSO. Team members use their existing company credentials to log in — no separate password management. When a team member leaves, revoking access in your identity provider instantly removes their Shoutly AI access. IT-managed, fully auditable.
SAML 2.0 • Okta • Azure AD • Google
📱
Dedicated account manager
A named person at Shoutly AI whose job is your account. Not a shared inbox. Not a rotating support team. One person who knows your clients, your workflows, your posting schedule, and your business goals. Available via direct email, WhatsApp, and monthly strategy calls. Onboards your team, trains new hires, and proactively flags issues before they affect your clients.
Direct line • Monthly strategy call
📈
Advanced analytics & custom reporting
Custom analytics dashboards per client with your agency’s branding. Automated monthly PDF reports sent directly to clients on your schedule. Cross-client performance benchmarking — see which clients are growing fastest and identify patterns to apply across your roster. Custom date ranges, custom metric sets, and raw data export via CSV or API for your own BI tools.
White-label PDF • Automated delivery • API export
📄
Client approval workflows
Send clients a shareable approval link — no Shoutly AI account required on their end. They review the month’s content calendar, leave comments on individual posts, approve or request changes, and get a notification when their content is published. Full approval audit trail: who approved what, and when. Removes the back-and-forth email chain from every client’s content review.
No client seat required • Full audit trail
🔁
API access & integrations
Full REST API access to integrate Shoutly AI into your existing marketing stack — CRM, project management, reporting tools, custom dashboards. Trigger content generation from external systems. Pull analytics data into your own BI platform. Push approved posts from your workflow into Shoutly AI’s scheduler. Webhooks for real-time event notifications. Dedicated API rate limits, not shared with standard plan users.
REST API • Webhooks • Custom rate limits
📋
Audit logs & compliance
Complete audit trail of every action in your account — who logged in, who created a post, who approved content, who published, who changed Brand Kit settings. 90-day log retention (extensible to 1 year). Exportable for compliance, internal review, or client dispute resolution. Essential for agencies where client trust and accountability are contractually required.
90-day retention • Exportable • Compliance-ready
💱
99.9% uptime SLA & priority infrastructure
Contractual 99.9% uptime guarantee. Enterprise accounts run on dedicated infrastructure — your publishing queue is never delayed by traffic spikes from standard plan users. Priority support queue with a maximum 4-hour response time during business hours. Scheduled maintenance windows communicated 72 hours in advance. For agencies where a missed post is a client service failure, this matters.
Contractual SLA • Dedicated infrastructure
📷
Custom AI training & brand voice refinement
Work directly with Shoutly AI’s team to fine-tune the AI for each client’s brand voice using examples from their existing content. For agencies whose clients have highly specific tonal requirements — legal firms, luxury brands, government bodies — this produces AI output that requires minimal human editing before approval, saving your team hours per client per month.
Custom AI • Per-client voice training
📚
Priority onboarding & team training
Dedicated onboarding sessions for your entire team — not a self-serve help centre and 3-minute tutorial video. A Shoutly AI specialist runs live training sessions for your team, sets up all client workspaces, configures Brand Kits for your existing clients, and gets your first month of content approved before your official go-live date. New team member joined? Your account manager trains them directly.
Live onboarding • All client setup included
