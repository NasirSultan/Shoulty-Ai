The design problem

Great content dies without great visuals. But most businesses can’t afford a designer.

Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn are visual-first platforms. A strong caption with a weak graphic gets ignored. The businesses winning on social have consistent, branded visuals — and most of them now use AI to get there.

⌛
Canva takes 30–45 minutes per graphic
Open Canva, find a template, change the text, swap the colours, fix the fonts, add your logo, resize for each platform, export, upload to your scheduler. That’s 45 minutes for one image. Multiply by 5 posts a week.
👤
Hiring a designer isn’t practical for daily content
Agencies charge ₹500–2,000 per graphic. A month of daily social media images could cost ₹15,000–60,000 in design fees alone — before a single word of copy is written or a post is scheduled.
🌌
Visuals look inconsistent across platforms
When you’re rushing or using free stock images, your Instagram looks different from your Facebook, which looks different from your Google Business Profile. Inconsistent branding erodes trust faster than no branding at all.
🎉
Festival graphics are always last-minute
Diwali is in 2 days and you still haven’t made a graphic. You grab a generic template, slap your logo on it, and publish something that looks nothing like your brand. The businesses with planned festival creatives outperform you by 3× on those days.
📷
Wrong image sizes break platform layouts
A 1:1 square image cropped for a 9:16 Reels thumbnail looks broken. A landscape banner uploaded as an Instagram feed post gets cut off. Every platform has different size requirements — and manually resizing for each one wastes another 10 minutes per post.
🖼️
Stock images feel generic and unbranded
Pexels and Unsplash images look the same on every business’s page. Your audience can tell immediately when a visual has nothing to do with your actual business. AI-generated branded graphics change this — every visual is specific to you.
