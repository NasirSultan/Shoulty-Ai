How to Request Data Deletion

Choose the option that works best for your situation. All methods result in the same outcome: permanent, irreversible deletion of your data from Shoutly AI’s systems.

1
Delete Your Account
If you have access to your account — fastest method
1
Sign in to your Shoutly AI account
2
Navigate to Settings
3
Select Delete Account
4
Confirm your deletion request

Your request immediately enters our deletion queue. You will receive an email confirmation when the process is complete.

Sign in to delete account
2
Request by Email
If you cannot access your account

Send an email to our support team. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7–30 business days.

To support@shoutlyai.com
Subject Data Deletion Request
Include Full name, registered email, company name (optional)

Our team may contact you to verify identity before processing.

Send deletion email
3
Meta / Facebook Data
If you signed in via Facebook or connected Meta accounts

If you used Facebook Login or connected your Facebook or Instagram account, request deletion of all Meta-related data stored by Shoutly AI.

To support@shoutlyai.com
Subject Facebook Data Deletion Request
Include Full name, registered email, Facebook account email

After verification, we permanently remove all Facebook-related data from our systems.

Send Facebook deletion request
💙
Remove Shoutly AI from Your Facebook Settings
Disconnects your Facebook account from Shoutly AI immediately. Submit a deletion request above to also remove your data from our servers.
1
Open Facebook
Log in to your Facebook account on the web or mobile app.
2
Go to Settings & Privacy
Click your profile icon (top right) and select Settings & Privacy.
3
Select Settings
From the dropdown menu, click Settings.
4
Open Apps and Websites
In the left-hand menu, navigate to Apps and Websites.
5
Find Shoutly AI
Locate Shoutly AI in your list of connected applications.
6
Click Remove
Select Shoutly AI and click Remove. This disconnects your Facebook account from Shoutly AI. To permanently delete your stored data from our servers, also submit a deletion request using one of the options above.
